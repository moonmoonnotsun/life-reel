What is Life Reel?

Life Reel: Photo & Video Diary is an iPhone app for keeping more memories — a calendar of your life, music-synced highlight videos, On This Day, trips, and Year in Review. Built for families, travelers, couples, and anyone who wants a video diary without uploading their life to the cloud.

Life Reel FAQ

Common questions about our photo & video diary for iPhone

What does Life Reel do?

You save photos and videos to days on a life calendar, then create highlight reels with music for any week, trip, month, or year.

Are my photos uploaded to the cloud?

No. Photos stay on your device. The app reads your library to show memories and only shares when you export or send a video.

Can I add my own music?

Yes. Use the built-in soundtrack library or add your own. Clips can sync to the music automatically.

Is Life Reel free?

Yes — free to download on the App Store. Premium is available as an optional in-app subscription.
